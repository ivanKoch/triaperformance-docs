## Review-generation playbook

1. **Milestone-triggered ask.** The July push proved the ask converts. Standardize: request a GBP review at race-goal-achieved, PB, or plan completion — the moment satisfaction peaks. One WhatsApp template, sent within 48h of the milestone.
2. **Targeted-language asks.** Every English- and Portuguese-speaking athlete and plan buyer gets asked, priority one, until English ≥10 and Portuguese ≥5. This directly de-risks the $149/US positioning and the PT catalog.
3. **Plans and All-Access asks.** Nurture-sequence email 8 (or a new final email) should ask satisfied plan finishers for a GBP review mentioning the plan. All-Access is too small to matter yet.
4. **Upgrade the 5 star-only reviews.** Light-touch ask to Jhon Jairo, Ronald Gonzalez, Paul Lehman, Ivan Schilaci, Santi Merlo to add one sentence.
5. **Never incentivize** — violates Google policy and the reviews are converting unaided.
