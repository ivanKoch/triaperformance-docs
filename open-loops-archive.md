## Closed — September 2, 2026

- [x] ~~**`Other: (not set)` is the largest single GA4 channel — 50 of 112 external sessions, 44.6%.**~~ *(Found in close #1.)* **CLOSED September 2, 2026. It was direct traffic, and the cause was a missing `NULLIF`.**

  **What it actually was.** *GA4 writes the **literal string** `(not set)` into `session_traffic_source_last_click.manual_campaign` for a session that arrived with no UTM tags — it does not write NULL and it does not write `''`.* `sync_ga4_data.py` *guarded with* `NULLIF(x, '')`, *which catches the empty string and passes* `(not set)` *straight through. The* `'(direct)'`/`'(none)'` *defaults therefore never fired, and* `ga4_channel_day`'s *Direct arm — which requires* `source = '(direct)'` — *never matched a single session.* **Fix: `NULLIF(NULLIF(x, ''), '(not set)')` on `source` and `medium`. Two lines.**

  🚨 **The probe that caused it was true and misleading, and that is the transferable part.** *The Aug 30 note read: "`manual_campaign` … a probe showed is populated on 100% of events." It is populated on 100% of events —* ***because the sentinel is a string***. *An `IS NOT NULL` check reports full coverage on a column entirely full of* `(not set)`. **A coverage check has to name the sentinels it tests against, or it measures the column's existence and reports it as the column's quality.** *Same family as this repo's other two: an aggregate log line that cannot distinguish "no matches" from "never ran", and a status file that reads as a quiet week.*

  **What the investigation also settled, and it is a decision not to change anything.** *`cross_channel_campaign` was compared against `manual_campaign` across the property's whole life. **They agree on every row except untagged/direct** (75 sessions where manual says `(not set)/(not set)` and cross-channel says `(direct)/(none)`).* ⚠️ ***So cross-channel is NOT a better source here, and `default_channel_group` is actively worse:*** *it labels this site's deliberate UTM convention —* `trainingpeaks/plan_listing`, `trainingpeaks/bio` — *as **"Unassigned"**.* **That is precisely what the `ga4_channel_day` header comment predicted in advance when it chose to derive channel from the site's own rules. The design was right and now has evidence behind it. Do not re-litigate it.**

  **The repair.** *Deleted the 108 affected rows (`source = '(not set)' AND medium = '(not set)'`) rather than the whole table — `ga4_traffic_day`'s unique key includes `source` and `medium`, so re-syncing changed values would have **missed the ON CONFLICT target and inserted duplicates alongside the originals**, silently double-counting every direct session. Then `--backfill --start 2026-07-21 --end 2026-09-01`, which reaches the property's entire life because the BigQuery export does.* **Verified: August totals 196 sessions in Postgres against 196 in BigQuery, and the duplicate check returned zero rows.**

  **August after the fix, external sessions (`ga4_channel_day` excludes internal):** *Direct 55 · Organic Search 34 · Profile / Bio Link 9 · TrainingPeaks Listing 7 · Google Business Profile 7 · Social 4 · Referral 2 —* **118 total, and `Other: (not set)` no longer exists.** *Nothing moved except the 50-odd direct sessions; organic, referral and every tagged channel were always being read correctly.*

  ⚠️ **One accepted cost, recorded so it is not discovered as a surprise.** *The fix maps **every** `(not set)` to `(direct)`/`(none)`, including the one August session that **GA4 itself** could not attribute (cross-channel also reported `(not set)`, which GA4 labels "Unassigned"). That session is now counted as Direct.* **This slightly weakens the view's own "an unmapped value must name itself" principle, at 1 session in 196.** *Judged not worth a third branch; revisit only if the truly-unattributable share ever grows.*

  🚨 **What the fix did not do: make Direct readable.** *Direct is now **46.6% of external sessions**, the largest named channel — and a 46.6% direct share on a site with negligible brand search is not a finding, it is a second bucket.* **It is some mix of untagged links Iván pastes by hand (WhatsApp strips referrers, and WhatsApp is where this business sells) and self-traffic the cookie-bound rules miss.** ***Which is why this closes and the internal-traffic item does not: that item's Sept 4 check gates whether this 46.6% can be believed at all.***
