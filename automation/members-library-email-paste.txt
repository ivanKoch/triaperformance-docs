WHY THIS IS SMALL ON PURPOSE
----------------------------
The three All-Access sales pages had to be rebuilt today because they hand-wrote
the tool list and decayed: they were still selling "flexibility, kettlebell,
fixing lower back pain" months after none of those existed.

The emails did NOT decay, and the reason is worth keeping: they never named a
single tool. They say "the calculators and guides, and I add new tools every
week", which stays true whatever ships.

*** So the rule going forward: emails name CATEGORIES and ONE durable starting
point. They never name the current line-up. The sales pages name everything and
are generated from site/_data/library.json. ***

The only change below is adding a first step. A subscriber currently gets a
password and a link with nothing telling them what to do first, which is the
difference between "I have access" and "I used it".

Both nodes live in: subscription-lifecycle-automation.json
