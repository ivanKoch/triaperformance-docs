Guardá. Node 2 ("Build Welcome Email") is already TUTEO. They have disagreed with
each other since August.

Iván's September 4 decision standardised Spanish to TUTEO (brand-guidelines.md §8).

worse than consistent voseo. So the new sentence cannot simply be dropped into a
voseo email.

Node 1's Spanish is therefore converted to tuteo in this same paste. It is six
markers in the whole node. They are listed individually below — do them all or
do none of them, because doing some is the failure mode.
