> ✅ **The most useful output of the five batches.** Verbatim questions with URLs, nothing invented, and — the part that matters most — **a recurring-theme count across all 18 races that gives the page template its section order.**
>
> ## The finding that changes the build
>
> | Theme | Races that actually ask it |
> |---|---|
> | **Kit: who can pick it up** | **16/18** |
> | No race-day kit pickup | 14/18 |
> | Time limit / intermediate cuts | 14/18 |
> | Corral / ola / cajón / caixa | 13/18 |
> | Transfer / name / distance change | 13/18 |

> | **Hills / "is it flat"** | **8/18** |
> | Transport to the start | 8/18 |
> | BQ / WMM / certification | 4/18 |
>
> **The course question — the one a coach assumes is the point — is asked half as often as who is allowed to collect your race kit.** Registration mechanics, kit rules and corral assignment dominate. That is counter-intuitive and it should decide the page order.
>
> **Proposed block order, same in every city:** registration reality → kit rules → start corral → course (hills/altitude) → cut-offs in clock time → how to reach the start. **A seventh Travel block only where runners do not sleep next to the start** — Lisboa (Cascais), Rio (Reserva), Mexico City (CU).
>
> ## Best individual material
>
> - **Rio, YouTube.** The cut-off question nobody answers, asked exactly: *"O tempo de corte da maratona é contado a partir da largada de cada pelotão? … se o tempo de corte for contado a partir da primeira largada, quem sai nos últimos pelotões acaba tendo menos tempo."* This recurs across races and no organiser addresses it.
> - **Lima, Instagram — nine comments, all the same confusion.** *"Aqui la Ola por calle. En el correo la Ola por colores. CUAL ES LA RELACION COLOR-CALLE?"* An organiser failure a good page simply fixes.
> - **Floripa, Instagram.** Runners contradicting the official "80% plano" line: *"a primeira parte tanto dos 21 e 42 k é bem complicada"*, *"Pessoal reclamando das subidas na prova, vem treinar em BH."* Official copy versus runner reality.
> - **SP City, YouTube.** *"largar as 6:00 já era meio absurdo, 5:30 então sem comentários, tem que acordar às 2:30 da manhã!"*
>
> ## Honest gaps, not padded
>
> **Bogotá 42K returned 7 and Porto 8**, both stated as short rather than filled out — and Bogotá explicitly refused to borrow the Media Maratón de Bogotá's FAQ, which is a different race. It also marks its own Yescom count as weak: *"hit 10 only by counting organiser copy the kit desk is written to answer."*
