# SuperHalfs — the series (§10, shared by all six)

*Series-level facts once, here. Each of the six dossiers below carries its own short `§10 — at this race`.*

*Researched 11 Sep 2026. Official = superhalfs.com FAQ/T&C/news, and the race organisers' own sites.*

**Membership.** Six races: Lisbon, Prague, Berlin, Copenhagen, Cardiff, Valencia. The original five (SH5) were "Lisbon, Prague, Copenhagen, Cardiff and Valencia". Berlin was added later: "From 2024, the GENERALI BERLIN HALF MARATHON will become an integral part of the SuperHalfs" (announced 11 Oct 2023). The series was planned for 2020, frozen for 2020/21 (COVID), and results count only from "the Prague Half Marathon on April 2, 2022" onward. For Berlin, "Berlin started in 2024". RunCzech describes it as "founded and headquartered in Prague".

### How the collection works
- **Passport.** You create a free account on superhalfs.com and get a SuperPassport, a 36-character ID. You enter that number in each race registration: "Always enter your SuperPassport number on each race registration." There is no dedicated app. The passport is a card you can add to Apple/Google Wallet.
- **Results matching.** "We will assign the results to the runners' accounts and you can track your progress on our site in your SuperPassport." If a result doesn't show up, "Most likely, there is an issue with your SuperPassport number or with your name."
- **You don't have to register before your first race.** Races run before signing up still count: "Don't worry, it doesn't matter at all! Register … and then go to the claims page and fill out a simple race results form" (bib number + chip time). The same applies if you entered a race before you had a passport number.
- **Reward.** "Every runner who completes the series will earn a SuperMedal, to be collected after finishing the final race." You get a Hall of Fame entry, the title **SuperRunner** ("If you finish your last race and your chip time is listed in results, then you become a SuperRunner"), and "The SuperPassport will be locked." You are told to "Stop by our booth at the Expo before the race to check that you are on the list." No certificate is mentioned (checked FAQ and T&C).
- **Counting rules.** Only official chip-timed results count. DNS/DNF/DQ and virtual races don't. You can't replace a city by running another city twice. **Trap:** "a newer result at the same race replaces the old one", so a slow repeat run overwrites your earlier time.
- **Time limit.** SH6: "From 2025 there is no time limit to complete your journey" (rule change of 11 Feb 2025; it was 60 months in 2024). SH5: "within 36 months, starting from the date you finish your first race … a rolling window."
- **Adding or removing races.** "The organisers reserve the right to evaluate potential candidates and to add additional races in the future. Or change or remove a race." Berlin shows how this was handled: SH5 runners could move to SH6 or opt out. If you finish the five you get one medal, and a second if you later run Berlin (SH6 Q&A PDF). If a race is cancelled, "the series would not be frozen."

### Entry for set-chasers
- **No SuperHalfs guaranteed entry in general.** The FAQ lists "three ways": the race's ballot or registration, a travel agency package, or charity. The only guarantee is the 2025 "stuck" rule: SH6 runners with at least 2 races done who haven't managed a race in two years get "a code or link for registration", subject to race capacity.
- ⚠️ Valencia's own SuperHalfs page lists "guaranteed registration" as a series benefit. That contradicts the series FAQ. **The FAQ wins.**
- **Agencies** get "no more than 5% of total entries" (SuperHalfs news, Jun 2025). Examples: Travelmarathon ES/BR, Sub4 (BR).
- **Charity** goes through realbuzz: "All partner charities have a limited amount of guaranteed entries in every SuperHalfs event". Each charity sets its own fundraising minimum.
- **SuperVoucher** (€295 for five races) was "discontinued in July 2023". Lisbon's 2027 ballot rules still let existing holders skip the ballot.
- **How hard each race is to get into:**
  - **Prague** is probably the hardest (inference). 2026 was first come, first served and sold out in "four hours", with "nearly 100,000" trying for 17,000 places (RunCzech). **For 2027 it moved to a ballot** (21 Jul–11 Aug 2026, results 21 Aug), and the ballot FAQ says SuperHalfs runners "must enter the ballot" — no priority ([RunCzech ballot FAQ](https://www.runczech.com/en/generali-prague-half-marathon-2027-ballot); see the Prague dossier).
  - **Valencia 2026** was a first-ever ballot: 50,766 applicants for 12,402 ballot places. That is ~24% odds (my calculation).
  - **Cardiff**: the 2025 race "sold out within hours". It went to a ballot for 2026, with a separate international ballot so there is "sufficient capacity … for those from overseas looking to complete the SuperHalfs series".
  - **Copenhagen**: lottery. 2026 (World Road Running Champs) sold out. The 2027 lottery runs 16–30 Sep 2026 and has a "SuperRunner" checkbox. No stated advantage.
  - **Berlin** and **Lisbon**: ballots. No applicant counts found.
  - Running Insights (Jun 2026): "all six SuperRaces now moving towards operating ballot entry systems."

**Fees.** "There is no cost to participate in SuperHalfs." T&C: "All Services are provided … free of charge." You only pay the races' own entry fees.

### Prominence vs the Abbott WMM (honest read)
**Verdict:** a real, fast-growing European and travel-runner product, but **roughly an order of magnitude smaller than the Majors in completions**. It is not obscure inside its own races, and it is thin in global and US discourse.
- **Completions (my estimate from Hall of Fame pagination, ~100 rows/page, sorted by races completed):** about **3,000 SH6 finishers** (6/6 runs to about page 31) plus about **720 SH5 finishers**. That's roughly 3,700 medals over 4 seasons. **Six Star finishers: 23,260** by end-2025 (Running Channel), and ">20,000" after Tokyo 2025 (AWMM official). So SuperHalfs has about 1/6 of the Majors' completions.
- **Registered passports:** "over 15,000 … from 84 countries" (AIMS, Jan 2023) → "over 75,000 runners joining in three years" (official, Feb 2025) → "more than 150,000" (Running Insights, Jun 2026; press, not official). The same article says 38% of 2025 finishers held a passport, and ">60%" in early 2026.
- **The six race sites:** all six mention the series. Dedicated pages exist on CPH, Cardiff, Valencia and RunCzech; Berlin and Lisbon have news posts.
- **Social / Reddit:** not measurable here. Instagram, X and Facebook refused fetching (robots). WebSearch returned **zero** Reddit threads, but that may be a limit of the search tool and doesn't prove there are none.

Men's WR **57:20** (Kiplimo, 8 Mar 2026) was set on the **elite** course, not the bridge course ([World Athletics](https://worldathletics.org/news/report/jacob-kiplimo-half-marathon-world-record-lisbon)).

### 2. Where runners actually struggle
- **km 15–21: running past the finish into an exposed out-and-back, with wind.** «the finishing 7 or 8km where you ran past the finish knowing that you still had to run out and back» — Roy K., 2025 ([Racecheck](https://racecheck.com/races/edp-lisbon-half-marathon/)). 2026 Portuguese champion Samuel Barata: «condições climatéricas que não favoreceram a parte final, devido ao vento» ([FPA](https://fpatletismo.pt/atletismo/geral/2026/03/samuel-barata-e-mariana-machado-campeoes-de-portugal-de-meia-maratona-em-lisboa/)). Mechanism: psychological (finish in sight at ~km 15) + riverside wind on one leg of the Dafundo turn.
- **Three U-turns on a narrow course.** «so many difficult U-turns»; «The course was far too narrow for the number of participants» — Ali M., 2025 (Racecheck). Each retorno is a brake-and-reaccelerate in a 19,000 field.
- **km 0–3: bridge congestion.** «so many fast runners pushing past as they were screwed at the start» — Roy K., 2025; «packed like sardines» at the start (2024 report). The first 3 km are gently downhill (report), which invites going out fast in traffic.
- **The last 40 m.** «it took 1½ minut to complete the last 40 meters!» — Jimmy T., 2025; «wait eight full minutes» — Ali M., 2025 (Racecheck). The 2024 reporter hit the first of two finish mats and stopped dead. Relevant because mass-start official time is **chip** time.
- **Taking the 10K lane by mistake.** «we almost ended up in the 10km section instead of the Half Marathon» — Max H., 2025 (Racecheck). Switching to the 10K route = DQ (Art. 2.º).

### 1. Course
Single loop, **start Jagtvej (Østerbro, between Vibenhus Runddel and Østerbrogade), finish Øster Allé by Fælledparken** ([2025 traffic release](https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/14576497/cph-half-fest-begraenser-trafikken-14-september?publisherId=12522877&lang=da)). **Course history:** new route in 2024 (Jagtvej start, "flatter and faster with more long stretches") ([2024 route](https://cphhalf.dk/en/er-du-klar-til-copenhagen-marathon-2024/)); 2025 unchanged; **2026 WRRC course "is the same as that of the Copenhagen Half Marathon in 2024 and 2025"** ([WA news](https://worldathletics.org/en/competitions/world-athletics-road-running-championships/copenhagen26/news/news/see-the-courses-for-the-wrrc-copenhagen-26); [Dansk Atletik](https://dansk-atletik.dk/nyheder/2025/03/her-er-ruterne-til-vm-halvmaraton-2026/)). 2027 start Jagtvej, finish "in the green heart of Fælledparken" ([course page](https://cphhalf.dk/en/alt-om-ruten/)).

**Record history — verify framing:** Kamworor's **58:01 (2019)** was the men's world record ([organiser facts](https://cphhalf.dk/en/facts-and-statistics-about-cph-half/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen_Half_Marathon)); Hassan set a European record in 2018. **Both were on the pre-2024 route**, not the current Jagtvej course. Women's course record **64:44**, Likina Amebaw, 2025 (current course) ([results archive](https://cphhalf.dk/en/results-archive/)). Certification: WA Gold Label since 2017 per Wikipedia; AIMS full member. Measurement certificate for the 2024–26 course: **not seen**.

### 2. Where runners actually struggle
- **The start, for anyone in the ≥1:50 groups — waiting, then running into midday.** 2026 wave release runs from mass gun 09:47 to **11:53 for "Over 2:15"**; 1:50–2:00 starts 10:56, 2:00–2:15 11:31 ([WA start groups](https://worldathletics.org/competitions/world-athletics-road-running-championships/copenhagen26/start-groups-halfmarathon)). A 2:15+ runner finishes after ~14:10. Reviewers: "Long wait times at the start to get moving if your expected finish is around two hours or slower" and "Late start at this time of year can make for hot running conditions" (2023, [RaceRaves](https://raceraves.com/races/copenhagen-half-marathon/)). Evidence is review-level, not measured.
- **Wet painted lines in turns (km 4–9 and throughout).** Rain is ~45% likely (climate normal, Marathon Index); in 2025 the eventual men's winner slipped on a road stripe in a turn after 7 km (official recap above). Mechanism: a dense, turn-heavy city loop run by 30,000+ on paint and wet asphalt.

### 1. Course

### 2. Where runners actually struggle
**Segment:** Fairoak Road, mile 12 (≈ km 19–20), just after the Roath Park Lake loop. **Mechanism:** a short, steep climb late in a race sold as flat. It catches runners who have held goal pace for 19 km. Evidence:
- The organiser's own Facebook post: "Mile 12. Fairoak Road. Not long to go, but a steep hill to overcome." ([FB post](https://www.facebook.com/CardiffHalf/photos/a.10150881749269205/10156886755824205/?type=3), quoted from a search-result title; the page itself could not be fetched).
- The organiser's A–Z (2018): "Twelfth mile, Fairoak Road… our mini version of hell". It says the incline "can really take your legs by surprise" ([A–Z](https://www.cardiffhalfmarathon.co.uk/z-cardiff-half/)).
- A 2022 runner report: "a nasty hill that we were warned about at Mile 12… there was no way I was running up the steep incline so we essentially had back-to-back walk breaks" ([Runner Beans](https://therunnerbeans.com/cardiff-half-marathon-is-this-the-best-half-in-the-uk/)).

**Secondary, with thinner evidence:**
- **km 0–5.** An early downhill in a 27,500-runner field. The same 2022 report says "We started off too quick in that first mile".
- **km 5–9.** Wind on the Barrage. This is a preview's warning (Sapphire), not a documented race-day problem.

The zone notes are from [Azana Running Lab](https://azanarunninglab.com/medio-maraton-valencia-recorrido-analisis/).
- **km 0–4.** Tarongers → Ronda Norte (km 1) → Alfahuir (km 2) → Primado Reig (km 3) → Botánico Cavanilles/Blasco Ibáñez (km 4). Wide avenues sized for 25,000 runners.
- **km 4–7.** Trinidad/Pont de Fusta (km 5) → Menéndez Pidal (km 6) → Fernando el Católico (km 7). This is the Turia bed and the Puente de Ademuz: crosswind exposure and "slight elevation gain" on the approach to the bridge (Azana, km 4.3–6.8).
- **km 7–11.** Ramón y Cajal (km 8) → Guillem de Castro/Hospital (km 9) → IVAM (km 10, the cut-off gate) → Conde Trénor/Puente Trinidad (km 11). Azana calls this favourable, economical terrain.
- **km 11–14.** Centre: Calle de la Paz (km 12) → Xàtiva/Plaza de Toros (km 13) → Navarro Reverter (km 14). This section has the most turns. WA's 2024 report cites "tight turns".
- **km 14–18.** Jacinto Benavente (km 15) → Alameda/Torre de França (km 16) → Menorca/Av. França (km 17) → Ingeniero Manuel Soto (km 18). Azana: "invita a dar más gas", the decisive section, and exposed to wind toward the maritime zone.
- **km 18–21.1.** Cabanyal: Eugenia Viñes (km 19–20) → Naranjos/Capitán Altobuey (km 21) → finish on Tarongers. Flat and fast, but mentally hard. Azana: the final straight is "más larga de lo que te va a apetecer".

**Elevation, do not average:**

- [DiarioTrail 2025](https://diariotrail.com/carreras/medio-maraton-valencia-2025-tiempos-y-cifras/): **35 m** cumulative.
- Organiser: **NOTHING FOUND** in the reglamento or course page text. The course PDF could not be fetched.
- Min/max altitude: **NOTHING FOUND**. The AEMET València station sits at 13 m.
- ([Carreraspopulares](https://carreraspopulares.com/noticia/medio-maraton-valencia-analisis-del-circuito)' 22 m is for an older course with a Marina finish. Do not use it.)

**Course change, last 2 editions:** none found. 2024 started and finished on Av. de los Naranjos ([El Debate 2024](https://www.eldebate.com/espana/comunidad-valenciana/valencia/20241027/horario-recorrido-cortes-trafico-media-maraton-valencia_238444.html)). 2025 used the same place, and 2026 repeats it. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_Half_Marathon)'s "starts on Carrer d'Antonio Ferrandis" describes an older layout.

### 2. Where runners actually struggle
**km 14–21, Alameda → Cabanyal → the Tarongers straight: sea wind arriving on the fastest-feeling section.**
- **Mechanism.** The course is flat. What goes wrong is the combination of a section that "invita a dar más gas" at km 14–18, wind exposure toward the seafront, and a long exposed finish straight. Azana: with a headwind "hay que ser inteligente: grupo, hombros relajados y esfuerzo estable". Azana also reports runners losing pace at km 18–20 on Cabanyal "despite favourable gradients".
- **2025 evidence.** WA: 20 °C, humidity >60%, and wind gusts that caused problems ([WA 2025](https://worldathletics.org/news/report/valencia-half-marathon-2025-ngetich-kejelcha)). [Carreras de Montaña](https://carrerasdemontana.com/2025/10/26/medio-maraton-valencia-2025-resultados-mayayo/): "un viento travieso que soplaba desde el mar", hurting the elites in the final kilometres.
- **2024 evidence.** Rain and tight turns (WA 2024). "Frío y lluvia intermitente" ([Carreras de Montaña 2024](https://carrerasdemontana.com/2024/10/27/medio-maraton-valencia-2024-resultados-mayayo/)).
- **Secondary.** The Turia bed and the Ademuz approach (km 4–7) carry crosswind early.
- **Caveat:** the evidence is elite reports plus one coach analysis. Mass-field pacing data and first-person popular race reports: **NOTHING FOUND**. The Reddit search was blocked by the proxy.

### 4. Cut-offs
Verbatim, [2026 reglamento, Art. 3](https://www.valenciaciudaddelrunning.com/medio/reglamento-21k-2026/):
> "La salida se dará a las 08:25 horas y la carrera finalizará a las 12:20 horas, siendo el tiempo máximo oficial para finalizar la carrera 2h45:00 desde su paso por la salida, sin que en ningún caso pueda excederse dicho lapso temporal, quedando fuera de carrera y de las clasificaciones oficiales los participantes que no cumplan con este límite."
> "Todo corredor que llegue al km 10 después de las 11 horas del 25 de octubre de 2026 deberá, obligatoriamente, entregar su dorsal, abandonar la prueba y/o subir al coche escoba."

- **Per runner, from the start mat.** The 2h45 runs from each runner's own crossing of the start line ("desde su paso por la salida"; the EN version says "since crossing start line"), not from the gun.
- **Absolute backstop.** The course closes at **12:20**. For the 09:27 wave, 2:45 means 12:12 (inference).
- **km 10 gate: 11:00 clock time, the same for every wave.** Inference: from 09:27 that allows 1:33 for 10 km, or 9:18/km. The 2:45 overall already needs ~7:49/km, so for a runner on pace the overall limit binds before the gate does.
- Sweeper car. No other intermediate gates found in the reglamento.
- [SuperHalfs FAQ](https://www.superhalfs.com/en/faqs/): Valencia's 2:45 is the tightest limit in the series.

### 2. Where runners actually struggle

### same_day_distances
**Not a standalone race.** It shares one weekend and one organiser with the **42K** and **10K** (both Sunday 6 Sep) and the **5K** (Saturday 5 Sep). The 42K is the same event and has its own dossier. Medellín has no second marathon to confuse it with.

- **Starts (reglamento 2026, horarios table):** 42K **5:00 a.m.**, 2 corrals on Av. San Juan. **21K 5:30 a.m.**, 3 corrals on Av. Carabobo. Both start from Parque de las Luces. 10K 8:30 a.m. from Parques del Río. 5K Saturday 7:00 a.m. All Sunday races finish at Edificio EPM, Parque de los Pies Descalzos.
- **Elite 21K start:** the timing lists give **04:59** ([Sportmaniacs](https://sportmaniacs.com/es/races/maraton-medellin-2026)) and **04:58** ([Spikes System](https://www.app.spikessystem.com/resultados/maraton-medellin)), alongside the 42K elite. This does not appear in the reglamento.
- **Shared course.** This comes from the official polylines in [routes.js](https://maratonmedellin.com/cdn/shop/t/14/assets/routes.js), the data behind the interactive map. It is **my inference; the organiser publishes no split point.**
  - Start points 21K `[6.245702,−75.573351]` / 42K `[6.245604,−75.57306]` (~30 m apart). Finish points are ~15 m apart.
  - **21K km 0 to ~15** uses the same roads as 42K km 0 to ~15: San Juan → Av. 80 → Carrera 70 → Calle 30 → south on Industriales / Las Vegas.
  - **The 21K U-turns at ≈km 15.7**, southernmost point `[6.20282,−75.57729]`, on Av. Las Vegas in El Poblado. That is **~1.3 km north of Glorieta Aguacatala**.
  - The closure table gives the Las Vegas west carriageway two schedules. **Calle 24 → Calle 1 Sur closes from 4:30**; **Calle 1 Sur → Aguacatala closes only from 6:00** ([Telemedellín](https://telemedellin.tv/maraton-medellin-2026-estos-son-los-cierres-viales/)). That places the 21K turn at about **Calle 1 Sur** (inference).
  - **21K km 15.7–21.1 is the 42K's last ~5 km.** 42K km marker 37 is at `[6.20226,−75.577238]`, the same spot as the 21K turn. 42K km 38–42 lie on 21K km 17–20.
  - The 42K passes the 21K turn outbound at ≈42K km 20.8 and goes on to Aguacatala and Sabaneta. Its km 15–19 markers also show an extra out-and-back on the same corridor that the 21K does not run.
  - **Net: essentially every road of the 21K is also a 42K road.** The 21K is the 42K minus its km ~15–37.
- **Cut-offs differ and have different starts.** FAQ 2026: «42K → 6:00 horas · 21K → 3:00 horas · 10K → 2:00 horas · 5K → 1:30 horas». Neither document says whether the clock is gun or chip. Measured from the gun, the implied closes are **21K 8:30** (5:30 + 3:00) and **42K 11:00** (5:00 + 6:00); this is inference. The 42K gates are **Sabaneta km 28.5 at 9:00** and **Aguacatala km 35.7 at 10:00**. Both sit on 42K-only road, off the 21K course.
- **Distance change:** the only rule published is «No es posible realizar cambios entre la distancia 5K y las demás distancias (10K, 21K o 42K), ni viceversa.» ([FAQ](https://maratonmedellin.com/pages/preguntas-frecuentes-2026)). Whether 10K↔21K↔42K changes are allowed, and at what cost or deadline, is **NOTHING FOUND** in reglamento Art. 4, the FAQ or [información general](https://maratonmedellin.com/pages/informacion-general-2026). The 2026 guide (read later — see addendum) adds nothing on distance changes.
- **Race-day switching is a DQ:** runners may not «suplantar personas ni correr una distancia diferente a la inscrita». «El incumplimiento de cualquiera de estas condiciones conlleva descalificación inmediata» (reglamento Art. 8).
- **Diversion rule: none.** A 42K runner who misses **Sabaneta at 9:00** falls under «deben abandonar la carrera o continuar por la acera bajo su propio riesgo» (reglamento 2026). The documents checked do not re-route anyone to the 21K, reclassify them as a 21K finisher, or mention a sweeper bus. That covered the reglamento, the FAQ, the 2025 guía and the 2026 guía (addendum). By then the runner is ~13 km south of the 21K turn, and the 21K close (8:30 if measured from the gun) has already passed.

### 1. Course
Starts at Parque de las Luces (21K corrals on Av. Carabobo) and finishes at Edificio EPM, Parque de los Pies Descalzos. **Out-and-back with a western loop.** «21.098 m (Media Maratón) … medidas y certificadas por World Athletics» (reglamento Art. 2). The organiser is an AIMS member. The official polyline measures 21.34 km (GPS, `dist` in routes.js).

Kilometres and heights below come from the organiser's own profile (`prof` in routes.js). Street names are my match against the km-marker coordinates and the closure table.
- **km 0–1.9** — Start, then west on Calle 44 San Juan across the river. Flat, 1,470 → 1,479 m.
- **km 1.9–3.8** — San Juan climbs west toward Av. 80, **1,479 → 1,526 m (+47 m, ≈2.5%)**. This is the only sustained climb.
- **km 3.8–6.9** — South on Av. 80, the course's high plateau at 1,525–1,531 m. The profile peaks at **1,530.8 m near km 5.3**.
- **km 6.9–12.6** — Turn at the Av. 80 / Carrera 70 junction (~km 8), north on Carrera 70, east on Calle 30 to Av. Industriales. Descends **1,525 → 1,479 m (−46 m)**.
- **km 12.6–15.7** — South on Industriales / Las Vegas to the U-turn. False flat up, **1,479 → 1,503 m (+24 m, ~0.8%)**.
- **km 15.7–21.1** — North on Las Vegas / Industriales (the east carriageway, per the closure table), then Carrera 54 / 55 to EPM. Descends to **1,466 m**. This stretch is the 42K's final ~5 km.
