### From that text alone

**(a) Missing km 28.5.** The only sentence that says what happens if you beat a time limit is: abandon the race **or** continue on the sidewalk at your own risk. Support is withdrawn; the course is no longer yours. That sentence is **not** the word *descalificación*. The only DQ clock in these clauses is the 15-minute late **start**. The text does not say you are pulled off the road, and it does not say sidewalk-at-own-risk is a DQ.

**(b) Clock or chip.** The gates are written as civil-clock times: «máximo 9:00 a.m.» / «máximo 10:00 a.m.» The text does not say «tiempo chip» or «desde su paso por la salida» for those two gates.

**(d) Starts.** One listed 42K start time: **5:00 a.m.** Two corrals behind that one time. Late to the corral → last corral, not a second gun. Wheelchair/other distances have other times; 42K does not.

### You were wrong — and so was a line in the batch-1 dossier

The 7:00/km figure was also bad arithmetic (28.5 km in 4:00 is about 8:25/km). I am not replacing it with a new “legal” pace. The published rules are the two clock gates plus a 6:00 duration next to a 5:00 a.m. start.
