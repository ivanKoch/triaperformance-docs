# 1. Boston Marathon
**Bank of America Boston Marathon (B.A.A.)**  
Official: https://www.baa.org/races/boston-marathon  
Next: **Monday 19 April 2027** (Patriots’ Day). 2026 was 20 April.

This is the page Medellín / Monterrey / Bogotá BQ attempts point at. Hitting the standard on a certified 42k is necessary and **not sufficient**.

## 1. Course, kilometre by kilometre

Point-to-point, Hopkinton → Boylston Street, Copley Square. Net drop ~137 m / ~450 ft (Hopkinton ~490 ft, finish ~10 ft). That net is the trap.

- **km 0–16 (miles 1–10):** downhill out of Hopkinton. First 4 miles drop ~267 ft (Runners Connect GPX). Framingham, Natick. Quads take the beating here.
- **km ~21 (mile 13):** Wellesley Scream Tunnel. Half.
- **km ~27 / mile 17.5:** right at the Newton firehouse onto Commonwealth Ave. Newton Hills begin.
- **Four Newton climbs, miles 16–21:** Firehouse, Cemetery, “the Ghost,” then **Heartbreak Hill** just past mile 20 (~88–91 ft over ~0.4–0.55 mi, ~3.3–4.5%). Strava segment 0.55 km, +25 m, 4.5% ([segment](https://www.strava.com/segments/3003495)).
- **After the crest:** Boston College downhill, Brookline, Citgo sign, right on Hereford, left on Boylston.

[Runner’s World 2026 breakdown](https://www.runnersworld.com/races-places/a71053261/boston-marathon-course-breakdown/). Certified. WA/AIMS/USATF — marks from here **are** BQ-valid (Boston is not a downhill-indexed course in the new 2027 penalty table; it *is* net downhill, but under the 1,500 ft / 457 m threshold).

## 2. Where runners actually struggle

Not Heartbreak in isolation. The data:

[Marathon Handbook, 110,013 finishers, 2022–2025](https://marathonhandbook.com/boston-mile-by-mile-pace-breakdown/): mile 20 is the slowest mile across four years, weather-invariant. 68% hit their slowest mile there. Only 18% still on goal pace. Average +31 sec/mi vs goal. Collapse window is miles 17–22, after the downhill has already wrecked the quads.

The coaching sentence that matches the file: people who treat Hopkinton like a tailwind pay in Newton.

**How a Latin American runner actually registers**

1. Run a **certified** marathon (WA / AIMS / USATF or national equivalent). Medellín, Monterrey, Bogotá count **if** that year’s course is certified — certification, not “it felt like a BQ race.”
2. Net (chip) time. Age on Patriots’ Day, not on the day you ran.
3. Window for 2027 opened **13 Sep 2025**. Apply **14–18 Sep 2026**, 17:00 ET Friday, in **Athletes’ Village**. Not first-come.
4. B.A.A. ranks by **how much you beat your standard**. Fastest buffers in first. That ranking *is* the cutoff, published after the window.
5. **New 2027:** ~1,000 **random** spots for people who hit the standard but missed the cutoff ([B.A.A. 22 Jul 2026](https://www.baa.org/news/registration-updates-and-information-announced-for-2027-boston-marathon-presented-by-bank-of-america/), Globe).
6. **Downhill index, first time 2027:** +5:00 if the qualifying course net-drops 1,500–2,999 ft; +10:00 if 3,000–5,999 ft; ≥6,000 ft not accepted. Most Latin American city 42ks are **not** in that penalty band (Medellín is up, not down). Check the specific GPX before you assume.
7. Charity / invitational / sponsor exist beside the qualifier pool. Same visa problem as any US race (see Travel).

A BQ from Medellín is a number on an application. It is not a bib.
